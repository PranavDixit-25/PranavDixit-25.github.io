The Developer Story

About Me

From hands-on robotics to engineering cognitive architectures and robust backend services.

I am a Computer Science and Engineering student specialising in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at UPES, Dehradun. I enjoy solving complex logical problems and building software that bridges practical, scalable systems engineering with modern artificial intelligence.

My journey in programming began after a summer robotics camp where I was introduced to Arduino coding. Seeing lines of code control sensors, servos, and physical devices in the real world made technology feel tangible, alive, and immensely exciting.

Around Class 10, that early fascination evolved into a deeper curiosity about AI, partly inspired by the idea of creating an intelligent assistant like JARVIS from Iron Man. That drive to turn sensory data, operations, and information flows into intuitive intelligence continues to shape what I learn and build every day.

Currently, I am deepening my expertise in AI model training, data pipelines, and agentic AI architectures while consistently building reliable backend systems, relational databases, and full-stack software.
